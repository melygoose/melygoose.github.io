Grove

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026.

Who uses Grove

Grove has one user: the person who built it. It signs in only to that person's own Google account.

What Grove accesses

  • Gmail, read-only. Grove reads the owner's messages to find receipts, bills and mail that needs a reply. It cannot send, delete or change mail.
  • Google Drive. Grove copies the owner's files to and from their own Drive as backups.

Where the data goes

Data from Google stays on the owner's computer. To sort mail, Grove may send message text to the AI model the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude), under the owner's own account. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Grove's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page.
